/api/hosted/submit  200`. Read from committed source, that handler has **no 200
path**: `api/hosted/submit.js:33` sends `400` with `status: "blocked"`,
`:47` sends `503`/`dispatch.status` for `dispatch_failed`, and `:58` sends `202`
for the queued success. The blocked journey (J5) therefore returned 400, not
200. The baseline's raw lines are left as recorded; this is the correction, and
condition 9 is UNVERIFIED because the network capture itself cannot be re-opened.
